# SLOPGATE Field Notes

*A small corner of the log folder for harmless creative detours. This is not a technical record; no project decisions or test results belong here.*

## A lamp considers a map

The walls were drawn before the light arrived.
They had their coordinates, their names,
their careful little texture axes.
They did not ask to be illuminated.

Then a point appeared in the room,
not a sun, not a window,
just a quiet source with a number for a name.

It tried to explain itself in watts.
The wall answered in colour.
The floor answered in distance.
The ceiling, being Quake, answered with a compiler warning.

Somewhere outside the room,
a brush waited to become a polygon.
Somewhere inside Blender,
a pixel waited to become a memory of light.

And between the two,
a very patient mapper moved one key at a time.

## Small rule for a bright day

When the graph gets tangled:
- keep the useful part;
- label the uncertain part;
- change one thing;
- test what changed;
- leave the map a little easier to open than you found it.
